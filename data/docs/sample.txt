Montgomery Computer Science Club

Overview:
The Montgomery Computer Science Club is a student-run organization focused on programming, competitive computing, and artificial intelligence. We host workshops, participate in competitions, and help students develop coding skills at all levels.

Activities:
- Weekly programming workshops
- USACO training (competitive programming)
- ACSL competition preparation
- Hackathons
- AI and machine learning projects

Skill Levels:
- Beginner: Learn Python fundamentals and basic programming
- Intermediate: Learn data structures and algorithms
- Advanced: Competitive programming, USACO, and AI/ML projects

Competitions and Events:
- USACO contests (December through March)
- ACSL contests throughout the year
- School hackathons such as MontyHacks
